# date-picker

## Do

- Explain the date format and allowed range, preventing selection of unavailable dates by mouse and keyboard.
- Distinguish the selected date, today and focus, and provide a way to enter a date without navigating the calendar.

## Don't

- Do not allow dates that contradict the task, such as future dates in historical reports.
- Do not change the selection when browsing months or shift the date through unintended timezone conversion.
