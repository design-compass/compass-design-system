# radar-chart-multiple

## Do

- State each dimension, direction and scale and keep axis order consistent across comparisons.
- Explain normalization when comparing different units and provide the original values.
- Limit the number of compared series and distinguish overlaps with lines, legends and transparency.

## Don't

- Do not interpret polygon area as a total score or reorder axes to make performance look larger.
- Do not force a radar chart when there are too many dimensions or overlapping labels.
- Do not let fills obscure another series or use different scales for each series.
