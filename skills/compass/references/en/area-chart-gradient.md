# area-chart-gradient

## Do

- Show magnitude and change along an ordered axis, with a clear baseline for the filled area.
- Label axis units and aggregation intervals and distinguish missing data from actual zero.
- Use the gradient only to support area identification; read values from the line and axes.

## Don't

- Do not connect unordered categories or let overlapping areas completely obscure another series.
- Do not fill gaps without explanation and imply continuous observation.
- Do not imply gradient intensity encodes a value or confidence level.
