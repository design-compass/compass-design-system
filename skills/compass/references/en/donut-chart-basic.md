# donut-chart-basic

## Do

- Use it for nonnegative parts of the same whole and identify that whole and its time period.
- Provide names, values and proportions, and choose a bar chart when small differences matter.
- Keep the center clear and calculate every segment against the same whole.

## Don't

- Do not combine overlapping categories or values belonging to different wholes in one pie.
- Do not create excessive tiny slices or distort angles and areas with 3D or tilt effects.
- Do not omit values because of the center hole or distort proportions with excessive segment gaps.
