# line-chart-step

## Do

- Show trends along an ordered axis such as time and clarify observation intervals and units.
- Use a shared scale for comparable series and distinguish missing values from actual zero.
- Use steps for values held between changes, such as a set price or inventory state.

## Don't

- Do not connect unordered categories and imply a trend.
- Do not hide axis breaks or irregular observation intervals that change how slopes are read.
- Do not place a step change at a time different from the recorded change.
